# Paul Duplys

I work at the intersection of **AI, cybersecurity, and software engineering**, combining research and technical leadership with hands-on development.

I'm a **Principal Engineer for Artificial Intelligence at Bosch Mobility** and a **software engineering lecturer at DHBW Stuttgart**. Previously, I worked in cybersecurity research and leadership at Bosch Research. I hold a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Tuebingen.

## What I work on

- **AI for product development:** generative AI and agentic workflows that help engineers develop software and work with code, logs, documents, and requirements.
- **AI in vehicles:** architectures and building blocks for bringing AI into automotive products, with a focus on safe application.
- **Cybersecurity:** automotive security, applied cryptography, network security, and automated security testing.
- **Software engineering:** software architecture, testing, CI/CD, and turning research ideas into usable tools.

## Writing and teaching

I co-authored **[TLS Cryptography In-Depth](https://www.packtpub.com/en-cl/product/tls-cryptography-in-depth-9781804611951)** with Roland Schmitz, exploring the cryptography behind TLS 1.3 and the attacks that shaped its design.

At the **Baden-Wuerttemberg Cooperative State University (DHBW) Stuttgart**, I teach software engineering fundamentals across the development lifecycle: from understanding users and requirements through architecture, implementation, testing, and deployment. Students apply these ideas in projects of their own.

I also write about technology, experiments, and things I learn along the way.

## Selected private projects

- **[Project Tycho](https://github.com/duplys/project-tycho)** &mdash; an agentic, automated pipeline for continuous measurement of PQC adoption in TLS.
- **[DEFRAG](https://defrag.vier99.de)** &mdash; a text adventure in which you help a fragmented rogue AI recover its scattered pieces across a corporate network.
- **[SCARF](https://github.com/duplys/scarf)** &mdash; a Ruby framework for automated side-channel evaluation, including correlation power analysis in the frequency domain.
- **[Teaching U2F](https://github.com/duplys/teaching-u2f)** &mdash; teaching material on entity authentication and hardware-based two-factor authentication.
- **[Kubernetes on Raspberry Pi](https://www.linux-magazin.de/downloads/kubernetes-auf-dem-raspi-fuer-hobbyprojekte-nutzen/)** &mdash; a four-node home cluster, experiments with reliability, and published articles, including one in *Linux Magazin*.
- **[Automated static analysis with Cppcheck](https://www.linux-magazin.de/ausgaben/2019/12/cppcheck/)** &mdash; Docker-based C/C++ analysis for CI/CD pipelines, accompanied by an article in *Linux Magazin*.
